[01] Principle

Evidence first. Claim second.

Three moves, one principle. Every output shows its source, signs itself, and can be checked independently.

A / CITE

Cite.

Every statement links to the source it rests on. A statement without a source is flagged, not hidden.

B / SIGN

Sign.

The content is fingerprinted, cryptographically signed, and given a trusted timestamp.

C / VERIFY

Verify.

Anyone can recompute the record and compare. No trust required. Checking is enough.

[05] Why now

The calendar, with its scope.

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) applies in stages. Dates may shift because of a proposed postponement package.

2026-08-02

Article 50. Transparency obligations.

Obligations such as marking AI-generated content and disclosing AI interaction.

2027-12-02

Annex III. High-risk systems.

The date for Annex III high-risk systems under the proposed postponement. It covers that category only, not every system.

2028-08-02

Systems embedded in products.

The date for high-risk systems embedded in regulated products.

Dates must be re-checked against official sources (EUR-Lex, European Commission) before publishing. This is not legal advice.

[07] Proof

What we do. What we don't claim.

Principle

  • Evidence comes before the claim.
  • Verification must work without trusting us.
  • What cannot be measured is not written.

Methodology

  • Content is digested with SHA-256.
  • The digest is signed and timestamped.
  • The record goes into a hash-chained, append-only log.
  • Accuracy measurements will be published with their method once they exist.

What we don't claim

  • We show that content is unchanged and where it came from, not that it is true.
  • There is no published accuracy rate, customer, award, or certification yet.
  • We give no legal advice and no compliance guarantee.
  • We do not use a blockchain.
