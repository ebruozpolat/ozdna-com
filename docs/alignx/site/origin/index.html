originDNA · ledger + attestation

The decision, on the record — and readable.

originDNA is the evidence layer of ozDNA. The Ledger writes every decision, vote, rationale and outcome to an append-only, hash-chained record (Article 12). The Attestation renders that record into something an auditor or regulator can actually read (Article 13).

Ledger · Art. 12 — record-keeping

Append-only, hash-chained

Article 12 asks for automatic event logs that are traceable and cannot be quietly altered after the fact. The Ledger chains each entry to the previous one by hash, so any later edit breaks the chain and is detectable.

APPEND-ONLY

Nothing is overwritten

Entries are only added. A correction is a new entry, not an edit — the original stays on the record.

HASH-CHAINED

Tamper-evident

Each entry carries the previous entry's hash. Change one and every entry after it no longer verifies.

COMPLETE

Decision + votes + rationale

The record isn't just the outcome — it's how the outcome was reached, including the Council's votes and any veto.
