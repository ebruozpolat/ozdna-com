# ozDNA — Claims you can check. It's in the DNA.

ozDNA is AI verifiability infrastructure, built by Kolaxa (Kolaxa Yazılım Teknoloji Sanayi
ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti., a Balıkesir University Teknopark company). One principle runs through
every product: AI output earns trust when the evidence travels with it. Cite. Sign. Verify.

## The two products

**ComplyDNA** (RegTech, Türkçe): citation-first compliance intelligence for Turkish
financial regulation (MASAK communiqués, AML/CFT laws 5549 & 6415, KVKK). Every sentence
in every answer links to the article behind it in the form `[TEBLİĞ / Madde]`, so
compliance teams can verify each claim at the source. Cloud API or fully on-premises.
Status: early access.

**OriginDNA** (content provenance, global): provenance for AI-generated content. Every
image gets a fingerprint, a signature at creation (open C2PA standard) and a timestamp
that anyone can verify independently. Built for EU AI Act Article 50 content marking
(applies Aug 2, 2026; grace for existing GenAI systems ends Dec 2, 2026). Status:
pre-launch, waitlist open.

## Research integrity

Kolaxa's academic platform tezmakale is moving towards evidence of AI use rather than AI
scores; ozDNA's provenance layer is the base for that direction.

## Separate line: AI oversight

https://ozdna.com/oversight/ describes a separate AI-oversight product line (EU AI Act
risk classification and human oversight). Its "complyDNA"/"originDNA" names refer to that
line, not to the two products above.

## What ozDNA is not

- Not an AI-text detector or humanizer; no detection-evasion products exist under this brand.
- OriginDNA is provenance, not AI detection; it has no token and holds no user funds.
- Outputs are information, not legal advice, and no compliance guarantee is given.
